lean-posix: test the record barrier with the helper held before it publishes

Found in item qgxw6v (the `record` spawn, `docs/decisions/0002-spawn-record.md`). The diff review (consult round 20261114-193656-9ed8, sol) noted that `Tests/Main.lean` `records` lets go of the caller's lock only after `trySpawn` returned, when the helper has already published and closed its copy, so it does not show that a separate opener is kept out while the helper has not yet written its line. The two parts are tested apart (`kept`: an inherited flock is held by the child's copy; `records`: the helper closes its copy before the program runs), not their interleaving. Proposed: a way to hold `helper/ctty.c` before `publish` that the tests control by an event (for example a test-only build of the helper that waits for a byte on an inherited pipe), then: close the caller's copy, check that `lockFile` on the record file says `lockHeld`, release the helper, take the lock and read the whole line. Costs a test seam in or beside the helper; no consumer contract changes.
