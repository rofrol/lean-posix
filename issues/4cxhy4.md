lean-posix: killSession can signal a process that took a member's pid between its second getsid and its kill: accept the window or find a fix

Found in item qgxw6v (commit "Add sessionMembers and killSession"). `Posix/Process.lean` `killSession` (C: `c/process.c` `kill_round`) watches a member's exit, reads its session again with `getsid`, then sends SIGKILL by pid. macOS has no pidfd, so a member that exits and whose pid another process takes between that `getsid` and the `kill` gets the SIGKILL instead; that needs the whole pid space to wrap in that window. Documented in the doc comment, not fixed. Consult round 20261114-192217-5853 (sol): "this API cannot honestly promise never signal an unrelated reused PID". The permission-failure path is tested only against notifyd's root-owned session (skipped as root), as no fixture of another uid exists without root; sol asked for a controlled fixture instead (rounds 20261114-192217-5853, 20261114-192512-b3b4).

Question for the owner: accept the window as documented, or look for a narrower mechanism (for example a task port or a `proc_pidinfo` unique id check, which still signals by pid)? And keep the notifyd test, or add a root-run fixture of another uid?
